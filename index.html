His Journey

I had bare knowledge of HTML, but the concept of actually making any website look like an art piece was completely
foreign to me. I had absolutely no knowledge of how to style at all, and even then, most of the HTML
intellect I gained was lost.
The Bio-Shield project truly got the ball rolling. I discovered how an actual website was structured,
and processed due to guidance from my teacher, peers, and the AI-written code I was moderating.
While Bio-Shield laid the groundwork for my advancement as a developer, EECU’s “Wise UP!” calculator
was where it all truly clicked into place, and I could write sites that could really shine with JavaScript Capabilities.
I feel as though this project is the shifting point I've been moving toward, almost bridging the gap between Web, and Game Design.
I really pray that my work, and passion is more than satisfactory.
I especially enjoyed learning about my peer’s way of designing, and developing a project. The way their lines of improvements
differed from mine. We also discuss our way of thinking, and construct upon each other. It also goes without saying that they’re some of the warmest,
and kindest people I've met.

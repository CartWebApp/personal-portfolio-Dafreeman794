I was tasked with a group to design the management of enrichment creation, deletion, editing, etc.
We heavily relied on both Figma’s intuitive design process, and demographic/user feedback to have a clean, intuitive layout for our section.

1. Resources

Due to time constraints, we could only manage a prototype.
Tools: Figma’s design tools, and functions.
Deployment: We shared our final draft to a variety of developers, and UX designers to receive feedback.

2. Skills I Acquired

Animal Enrichment Tracking: I experienced Firsthand how Zoo Care Specialists Keep track of the needs, and stimulating activities it needs.
Managing Personality Types: I learned how to merge skillsets with a variety of personalities differing from my own.
Design Feedback: I was able to advance in how to receive constructive criticism in every aspect of our design.

3. Problems Faced

The Problem: Our group struggled with the simplicity of our design. Some felt it would not contain enough information, while others thought it contained too much.
The Solution: we decided it would be best to have a simplistic layout, but fill it with enough data the specialists can apply.
The Problem: I pitched a simple Navbar button at the very start to log new enrichment for convenience's sake. My group was conflicted on it, since that wasn’t necessarily our primary directive for the project. The Solution: We ended up discussing, and continued work on the feature, but we labeled it as Priority 1, and focused our attention on perfecting our layout.

4. Mindfulness

Kindness: I not only desired to make my companions feel heard, but valued. Communication: I sought feedback not only from the Specialists themselves, but also pitched to my teammates open critical thinking, as did they to me.
